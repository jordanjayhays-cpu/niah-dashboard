# Amigo Sales Campaign Spec: Niah Franchise Discovery

## Objective
Book 15 minute discovery meetings with franchise development decision makers to see if Niah can help them get qualified franchisee candidates. Success metric: meetings booked with Jordan (jordan@niahconnects.com).

## Cadence
One email per week, four weeks total, per lead. Stop immediately on any reply. Send window: 9:00 to 11:30am in the lead's own time zone, Tuesday to Thursday.

## Audience
The 144 lead list (segments: search_lead = franchisor brand staff, agency = franchise sales agencies, expo_exhibitor = brands that exhibited at franchise expos). LinkedIn-only leads (no verified email) are handled on LinkedIn per the earlier brief.

## IMPORTANT: channel ownership
This email campaign already exists, fully loaded, in Instantly (3 campaigns: EAST / CENTRAL / WEST, currently paused). EITHER Instantly sends it OR Amigo sends it. Never both. If Amigo takes email, the Instantly campaigns stay paused permanently. LinkedIn outreach (Amigo) runs in parallel either way, per the LinkedIn brief: no double-asking in the same 2 days.

## The four emails

Variables: {greeting} = "Hi <first name>," or "Hello,". {opener} = segment-specific first line (expo leads: "I saw <company> exhibited at <show>." / search leads: "I am researching how emerging franchise brands actually find franchisees." / agencies: "You place franchisees for a living, so you know the real economics better than anyone."). {company} = company name.

### Week 1 — the pain
Subject: expo leads "your booth at <show>" / brands "finding franchisees" / agencies "what a placement really costs"

{greeting}

{opener} Most emerging brands tell us the hard part is not interest, it is getting qualified candidates in front of the right person. How is that going at {company}?

I work with Niah. We connect the right people at events and introduce them with reasons, over WhatsApp, so the right candidates reach you instead of walking past. Would you be open to a 15 minute call to see if that could help {company}?

Jordan Hays
Niah

### Week 2 — the proof (same thread)
{greeting}

Quick follow-up. In our pilots, 81 percent of the introductions we made were accepted, because every introduction comes with the reason those two people should meet.

For a franchise brand, that means candidates matched to you before and during an expo, instead of hoping the right ones find your booth.

Worth 15 minutes to see if it fits how {company} recruits?

Jordan

### Week 3 — the concrete moment (same thread)
{greeting}

Franchise Expo South runs February 5 and 6 in Fort Lauderdale. Imagine every serious candidate who registers getting a WhatsApp introduction to the brands that actually fit them, with the reason spelled out.

We are putting together a small group of brands for that pilot. If you would like {company} considered, a 15 minute call is enough to see if it makes sense.

Jordan

### Week 4 — the close (same thread)
{greeting}

Last note from me. If the franchisee pipeline at {company} is full, I will close the file, no hard feelings. If it is not, 15 minutes is all I am asking to show you how Niah gets the right candidates in front of brands like yours.

Either way, thanks for reading these.

Jordan

## Reply handling
- Any positive or curious reply: propose 2-3 concrete times (EST business hours) or send Jordan's booking link, same day.
- Questions about Niah: answer in 2 sentences max (event matchmaking over WhatsApp, introductions with reasons, 81 percent acceptance in pilots), then return to the meeting ask.
- "Not interested": thank, stop, mark closed. No rebuttals.
- Out of office: pause that lead 1 week, resume.
- Every reply is flagged to Jordan the same day with the verbatim text.

## Rules
- Text only, no links except a booking link in replies, no attachments, no images.
- Max one thread per lead. Weeks 2-4 are replies in the same thread.
- Respect daily volume: max 20 sends per sending inbox per day.
- Do not email anyone who replied, bounced, or unsubscribed. Honor opt-outs instantly.
