# Amigo Sales Brief: Niah Franchise Research Campaign (LinkedIn)

## What this is
Customer discovery (Mom Test) for Niah. Goal: book 15 minute research interviews with franchise development people at emerging franchise brands. This is NOT a sales campaign. Never pitch Niah. Never send a deck or a link. The ask is always a short research conversation about how they find franchisees and what it costs.

## Channel split (important)
- Email is owned by Instantly (3 campaigns already running: EAST / CENTRAL / WEST). Amigo must NOT email anyone on this list.
- Amigo owns LinkedIn only: connection request + one follow-up message after accept.

## The list: niah-amigo-linkedin-list.csv (184 people)
Columns:
- linkedin: profile URL
- segment: "search_lead" = works at a franchise brand (franchisor). "agency" = works at a franchise sales agency or broker network (different questions, see copy in file).
- in_email_campaign: YES = this person is also getting the email sequence via Instantly. NO = LinkedIn is the ONLY channel for them (67+2 people, no verified email exists).
- connection_note: ready-to-send connection request text (under 300 chars)
- followup_message: ready-to-send message after they accept


## Channel wording by region (important)
Niah introduces people through messaging. How we SAY it depends on the lead's region:
- North America (this campaign): always say "text message". Do not say WhatsApp to US or Canadian leads.
- Europe and Latin America (future campaigns): say "WhatsApp" — that is what they use and what the product runs on there.
This is wording for the audience, not a product rule.

## Rules
1. Priority order: start with in_email_campaign = NO (LinkedIn is their only door), then YES rows.
2. For in_email_campaign = YES rows: wait at least 2 days after the campaign start before the connection request, and never reference the email.
3. Volume: max 20 connection requests per day per LinkedIn account (deliverability and LinkedIn limits).
4. If someone replies with an answer or books a call: stop all follow-ups, flag to Jordan same day.
5. If someone asks "what is Niah": one sentence only ("We match people at events by text message, but this research is about your world, not our product") and return to the question.
6. No pitching, no links, no attachments, no more than one follow-up after accept. A second follow-up only if they engaged.

## What a successful contact produces
A 15 minute call booked with Jordan (jordan@niahconnects.com), or answers in chat to:
- At your last expo/event, how many booth conversations became signed franchisees?
- What does a signed franchisee cost you today (brokers, portals, expos)?
- (Agencies instead): what is your average all-in cost per successful placement?

Log every number quoted and every verbatim pain quote.
